00 · Heroic Raid Preparation

Before the First Pull

  • Require flask, food, weapon oil/stone, health potion and combat potion.
  • Install a current boss-mod package and Method Raid Tools or equivalent cooldown planner.
  • Assign two alternating soak teams before Sentinels, Sszorak and Coiled Altar.
  • Assign three Feast groups before Twin Fangs.
  • Assign two equal side/Coil groups before Ula'tek.

Progression Rules

  • Personal defensives are part of the strategy, not emergency extras.
  • Do not release until the raid leader calls the wipe.
  • Prioritize assigned adds, soaks and interrupts over boss damage.
  • Log pulls and review the first lethal event, not only the final death.
  • Branch order after Nek'zali is flexible; clear both wings before Twin Fangs.

01 · Nek'zali the Soulcoiler

ADD CONTROL · HEALING CHECK · BURN PHASE

Keep every Restless Amani away from the Soulcoil Well, clean up corpses during the 50% intermission, then burn Nek'zali before permanent Soulcoil Rite stacks overwhelm the raid.

Heroic changes: Soulcoil Ignition also applies Ritual Burn; the raid must enter the next Ignition with no lingering stacks. Defeated adds leave Vessel of Awakening corpses that must be burned during Hungering Pyre. Possession Barrage echoes punish anyone in their path.

Fight Flow

  1. Tank near the largest glowing coffin cluster so Restless Amani enter cleave range.
  2. Magic damage breaks their shields; then hard-focus them before they reach the central well.
  3. Essence Rend targets move to the wall before a healer dispels; the dispel leaves a Latent Cultist.
  4. Active tank runs directly away during Possession Barrage; everyone clears the line.
  5. Soulcoil Ignition: dodge echoes and use a healing cooldown. Stabilize Ritual Burn fully.
  6. At 50%, kill Echoes of Jawae one at a time. Place Hungering Pyre soaks over corpse piles.
  7. When Nek'zali returns, Bloodlust and burn. Invoke makes Soulcoil Rite permanent; adds still cannot reach the well.

Tanks

  • Swap around 5–6 Hollowing Strikes.
  • Move boss to coffin clusters.
  • Barrage tank runs away; other tank controls adds.
  • Intermission: position Pyre over corpses.

Healers

  • Cooldown every Ignition overlap.
  • Delay Essence Rend dispels until targets reach wall.
  • Clear Ritual Burn before next Ignition.
  • Ramp each Phase 2 Invoke.

DPS

  • Magic DPS break add shields first.
  • Grip/control strays away from well.
  • Focus Echoes sequentially.
  • Phase 2 is a boss burn without abandoning adds.
Biggest wipes: one shielded add reaching the well; early Essence Rend dispel; anyone between boss and Barrage tank; entering Ignition with Ritual Burn; corpses surviving intermission.
CALL: Coffins → shields → Rend to wall → clear Barrage line → heal Ignition → at 50% burn corpses with Pyre → Lust on return.

02 · Entombed Sentinels

SPLIT RAID · BALANCED HEALTH · ALTERNATING SIDES

Divide the raid between Blood and Acid, keep the Sentinels at least 40 yards apart, balance their health and swap sides after each intermission so mark stacks expire.

Heroic changes: Blood effects leave persistent puddles—drop them at the edge or on existing puddles. Destroyed Toxic Droplets send Living Venom back toward the Sentinel; move out of the return path.

Assignments & Flow

  1. Create two balanced groups, each with a tank and healers. Mark the two boss positions 40+ yards apart.
  2. Do not cross sides during a main phase; Mark of Acid/Blood damage ramps.
  3. Keep boss health close. During intermission, the lower-health Sentinel heals through Vitriolic Stasis.
  4. Blood side drops every puddle neatly at edges; do not consume the room.
  5. Breath of Ula'tek: soak Toxic Droplets before they explode, then sidestep their returning Living Venom lanes.
  6. Kill Venom Coagulation immediately.
  7. After every intermission, tanks and raid groups swap to the opposite Sentinel.

Tanks

  • Maintain 40+ yard separation.
  • Swap bosses after every intermission.
  • Use mitigation for escalating Empowering Slam.
  • Do not drag a boss through puddle lanes.

Healers

  • Split evenly between groups.
  • Cooldown late in each mark sequence.
  • Track soakers and Slam tank.
  • Stabilize before crossing after intermission.

DPS

  • Balance boss health continuously.
  • Soak assigned droplets.
  • Dodge the return paths.
  • Hard-swap to Venom Coagulation.
Biggest wipes: bosses inside 40 yards; unbalanced health; blood puddles through the center; missed droplets; standing in returning Living Venom; a group crossing early with both marks.
CALL: Split and stay → balance health → blood to edge → soak droplets → dodge return → kill coagulation → swap sides after intermission.

03 · The Lost Explorers

COUNCIL · FISH CONTROL · SOAK TEAMS

Control three independent health bars without stacking all three bosses. Break Gebbo's crates, collect fish and choose the next ultimate before Mor'zahi completes Final Ascension.

Heroic progression: Mighty Thud requires predetermined alternating soak teams; overlaps and missed soaks are lethal. Use the stable ultimate order Gebbo → Nama → Iku.

Fight Flow

  1. Bloodlust on pull. Keep two bosses together for cleave but never all three within 30 yards—United Defense gives 99% damage reduction.
  2. Melee stay near Nama and bait Shell Spin toward the outside.
  3. Interrupt Icebound Flames; Blink Nova targets move away.
  4. Destroy Throw Junk crates, pick up Grab Fish and feed the chosen explorer around 90–95 energy.
  5. Gebbo ultimate: bombs to edge away from mushrooms; enter mushrooms only as Blast Wave reaches them.
  6. Nama ultimate: one Mighty Thud in melee and two separated at range. Team 1 soaks sets 1/3; Team 2 soaks set 2.
  7. Iku ultimate: spread Frostfire circles at edge, then clear with the opposite type one at a time under healing cooldowns.

Tanks

  • Keep exactly two bosses together.
  • Rotate Nama between Gebbo and Iku.
  • Swap after Shredding Shards.
  • Keep bosses out of ultimate safe zones.

Healers

  • Plan cooldowns for Iku clears.
  • Top Mighty Thud targets before leaps.
  • Watch isolated bomb and Nova targets.
  • Do not clear multiple Frostfire circles together.

DPS

  • Balance health and follow the focus target.
  • Interrupt Icebound Flames.
  • Break crates and deliver fish on call.
  • Use assigned Thud team only.
Biggest wipes: all three bosses stacked; fish delivered late or to wrong boss; Shell Spin through raid; Thud circles overlapping; wrong soak team; simultaneous Iku clears.
CALL: Two together, one out → crates/fish → Gebbo bombs/mushroom → Nama alternate soaks → Iku opposite-color clears.

04 · Vashnik the Malignant

FOUNTAIN ROUTE · ADD PRIORITY · HEROIC SOAK

Position Vashnik between the two fountains intended for Imbibe, rotate the infusion combinations and kill every venom add before it reaches the central Malignant Cavity.

Heroic changes: soak Catalytic Bile. Burning Venom deaths apply a Caustic Surge DoT, so stagger their kills rather than detonating both together.

Recommended Route

Fire + Shadow → Fire + Blood → Blood + Shadow, then repeat as needed. A fountain infusion lasts 90 seconds and buffs its associated damage/add health, so avoid repeatedly empowering the same pair.

  1. Bloodlust on pull. Loosely stack near boss.
  2. Plague Froth targets aim their four cardinal lines away from raid.
  3. At Imbibe, tanks place boss decisively close to the selected pair of fountains.
  4. Add priority: Burning Venoms → Clotting Venom → Shrouded Venoms. Grip/knock adds together for cleave.
  5. Stagger Burning Venom deaths to prevent overlapping Heroic DoTs.
  6. Exploding Infection runs far away; Siphoning Infection is stacked/healed; Stygian Infection spreads and moves from zones.
  7. Assigned players soak Catalytic Bile.

Tanks

  • Swap each Dripping Fangs.
  • Control the fountain pair before Imbibe.
  • Keep adds from central pool.
  • Communicate if boss is not close enough to intended fountain.

Healers

  • Top Exploding Infection runners.
  • Dispel them after they reach range if useful.
  • Cooldown staggered Caustic Surges.
  • Heal Siphoning Infection stack group.

DPS

  • Hard swap Fire → Blood → Shadow.
  • Do not kill two Burning Venoms together.
  • Control split Blood adds.
  • Soak Catalytic Bile assignment.
Biggest wipes: incorrect fountain pair; an add reaches center; double Burning Venom death; Exploding Infection detonated in raid; Froth lines through stack; missed Catalytic Bile.
CALL: Set fountain pair → Froth away → Fire adds first and stagger deaths → Blood → Shadow → infections correctly → soak Bile.

05 · Sszorak

WIND MEMORY · CYST PLACEMENT · TWO SOAK TEAMS

Read the wind-tunnel order before each main phase, drop Viscous Cysts opposite those winds, and use each cyst during Howling Maelstrom to bounce safely toward the center.

Heroic changes: Mutilate gives soakers 500% increased damage from the next one—use two alternating teams. Caustic Claws creates Caustic Residue during Venomous Surge; tank near the outer edge so intermission winds remove the pools. Residue increases damage taken by 30%.

Fight Flow

  1. On pull and after intermission, identify active wind tunnels; raid leader marks cyst drop spots opposite them.
  2. Venomous Surge targets place one cyst at each marker—never double-drop.
  3. Tank Sszorak away from cyst markers and place Caustic Residue near the edge.
  4. Apex Predator: assigned team soaks Mutilate; dodge Tempest tornadoes. Alternate teams each cast.
  5. Raging Crosswinds: pair with a player pushed in the opposite direction so you collide and return safely.
  6. At intermission, move center and use cysts in wind order, standing so each knockback returns the group toward center.
  7. Boss takes 30% increased damage during Dig In—use planned burst.

Tanks

  • Swap around 6 Corroding Venom stacks.
  • Place Residue at edge.
  • Keep boss away from cysts.
  • Call active Mutilate soak team.

Healers

  • Track Team A/B Mutilate vulnerability.
  • Top Surge targets before drops.
  • Cooldown intermission movement.
  • Watch players touching Residue.

DPS

  • Memorize wind order.
  • Place one cyst per marker.
  • Only assigned group soaks Mutilate.
  • Pair opposite Crosswind arrows.
Biggest wipes: cyst placed on wrong side; doubled cyst; same players soaking consecutive Mutilates; Residue through center; no Crosswind partner; taking a cyst bounce away from center.
CALL: Read winds → drop cysts opposite → Residue edge → alternate Mutilate teams → pair Crosswinds → center → ride cysts in order.

06 · The Twin Fangs — Vexhul & Ithraz

VENOM STACKS · THREE FEAST GROUPS · TANK SEQUENCE

Mechanics add Eternal Venom; Ravenous Feast removes it. On Heroic, divide the raid into three groups and send each group to its assigned Feast soak.

Heroic requirement: three Ravenous Feast groups. Track Eternal Venom constantly and never approach the lethal threshold. The tank must remember and enter the three Stone Breaker soaks in their displayed order.

Fight Flow

  1. Bloodlust on pull and cleave both bosses.
  2. Caustic Globules must be soaked as assigned, adding one Eternal Venom stack.
  3. Kill Venomous Emergence adds before they distribute extra stacks.
  4. Dodge Stir the Depths waves; each hit adds a stack.
  5. Coiling Ichor targets drop pools at the outer edge.
  6. Ravenous Feast: Groups 1, 2 and 3 enter their separate soaks to remove stacks.
  7. Stone Breaker tank watches the order of the three indicators, then enters all three in that exact order to prevent raid damage.
  8. During Submerge, watch the center ring and dodge the laser traveling toward the indicated orb.

Tanks

  • Keep bosses positioned for cleave.
  • Stone Breaker tank takes all three in correct order.
  • Use strong mitigation for the sequence.
  • Do not drag bosses across Ichor pools.

Healers

  • Track venom stacks and Feast groups.
  • Top Stone Breaker tank.
  • Cooldown add/wave overlaps.
  • Call players near lethal stack count.

DPS

  • Know your Feast group.
  • Soak only assigned Globules.
  • Hard swap emergence adds.
  • Dodge waves and Submerge laser.
Biggest wipes: missed Feast group; high-stack player skips reduction; uncontrolled Globules; add lives too long; Stone Breaker order missed; Ichor through center; Submerge laser hit.
CALL: Track stacks → assigned Globules → kill adds → dodge waves → Ichor edge → three Feast groups → tank takes 1-2-3 order → dodge laser.

07 · The Coiled Altar

THREE PHASES · ORB/ADD CLEARING · ALTERNATE GUILLOTINE

Phase 1 fights Zul'jan, Phase 2 Malacrass, and Phase 3 combines them. Throughout the fight, cluster venom or manifestations and aim the tank frontal through them to clear space.

Heroic changes: destroying venom orbs applies a stacking raid DoT; axes persist; Guillotine soakers gain 500% vulnerability, requiring two alternating teams; Spiteful Soulcoilers cast a raid-wide fear and must be interrupted.

Phase 1 — Zul'jan

  1. Stack behind boss. Heal Fangs of the Coiled Altar and prepare for empowered tank swings.
  2. Toxic Deluge creates Coalesced Venom. Mobile carriers pick up orbs and drop them in one compact pile.
  3. Tank aims Sever through the pile. Do not clear too many simultaneously under existing DoT stacks.
  4. Guillotine requires at least five soakers. Team A/B alternate, then everyone runs beyond the 50-yard explosion band.
  5. Persistent axes make positioning progressively tighter.

Phase 2 / Intermission

  1. Control Manifestations of Dread and aim the appropriate tank frontal through them.
  2. Interrupt every Spiteful Soulcoiler fear.
  3. During the damage-amplification intermission, block souls from reaching bosses and burn.

Phase 3 — Combined

  1. Stack the two bosses for cleave while repeating orb and manifestation clearing.
  2. Grim Guillotine uses the same alternating soak teams and adds a healing absorb.
  3. Blighted Sever clears both Coalesced Venom and Manifestations; tank swap on its vulnerability.
  4. Defiled Ground absorbs healing—move immediately. Use remaining raid cooldowns for combined overlaps.

Tanks

  • Aim Sever/Blighted Sever through piles.
  • Swap every vulnerability application.
  • Prepare for post-Fangs melee spikes.
  • Keep bosses stacked in Phase 3.

Healers

  • Plan orb-clear DoT cooldowns.
  • Track Guillotine healing absorbs.
  • Do not heal from Defiled Ground.
  • Save major CDs for Phase 3 overlaps.

DPS

  • Move orbs into compact piles.
  • Alternate Guillotine A/B.
  • Interrupt Soulcoiler fear.
  • Block souls in intermission.
Biggest wipes: mass-clearing too many orbs; same Guillotine group twice; standing within 50 yards after soak; missed fear interrupt; souls heal bosses; frontal misses the pile; standing in Defiled Ground.
CALL: Stack behind → group orbs → frontal clears → alternate Guillotine and run far → interrupt Soulcoiler → block souls → combine everything in P3.

08 · Ula'tek — Final Boss

EGG CONTROL · TWO COIL GROUPS · SHRINKING PLATFORM

The encounter is governed by egg control: safe hatches create manageable Rawlings, while venom touching eggs or carriers creates dangerous Vipers and permanent additional raid pressure.

Current Heroic rules: Soul Constrictor prevents the same players from mitigating consecutive Spectral Coils—alternate two groups. Coils require 40% of the raid for minimum damage. Grasping Fangs targets three players per side and each break applies raid-wide Blight Vein; break controlled pairs and let the DoT expire. Volatile Purge radius scales with raid size.

Phase 1 — Egg Control & Heart Burn

  1. Keep a tank in melee at all times; losing melee contact triggers severe punishment.
  2. Dodge Caustic Waves and keep them away from eggs/carriers.
  3. Pick up eggs with Malignant Shell and hatch on schedule; kill Vipers immediately if a bad hatch occurs.
  4. Soak Spectral Coils with assigned Group A/B rotations.
  5. During Rage of the Shackled, dodge debris and burn the exposed Venomous Heart. Use Bloodlust on the first planned amp.

Phase 2 — Split Sides

  1. Two preassigned groups go to opposite sides and kill each Doomscale Warden in the corridor while cleaving Clutches.
  2. Grasping Fangs: snap two at a time across the encounter, wait for raid-wide Blight Vein to expire, then snap the next pair.
  3. After Warden dies, collect every egg. Feed small eggs to the Doomscale Cauldron first and the large Doomscale Egg last.
  4. Kill Weakened Doomscale, interrupt Anguished Cry and face Desperate Thrash away.
  5. Return to main platform and regroup for the next Heart damage amp.

Intermission — The Shattering

  1. Pick up every egg while moving around the platform.
  2. Group A soaks Spectral Coils 1/3/5; Group B soaks 2/4/6.
  3. Run tail-to-tail and ensure at least 40% of raid is in each assigned soak.
  4. At the shatter, avoid the center and use the knock-up to reach the four Phase 3 platforms.

Phase 3 — Ula'tek's Ascension

  1. Melee/tanks occupy boss platform; ranged/healers position counter-clockwise.
  2. Call of the Serpent spawns Clutches on platforms; ranged focus together, melee handle theirs.
  3. Serpent's Bite targets one melee, one healer and one ranged. Three groups fully soak their matching Bite, then immediately spread for Volatile Purge.
  4. If Bite is not handled, a lethal Calcified Corpse remains.
  5. Circling Prey destroys a platform: evacuate fully before it breaks.
  6. Taunt and kill Blightscale Shriekers; never exceed two tank DoT stacks and interrupt Vicious Echoes.
  7. Second platform break is the hardest overlap—commit remaining cooldowns. Final platform is an enrage race.

Tanks

  • Never leave Ula'tek without melee contact.
  • Pick up Wardens, Doomscales and Shriekers.
  • Face Desperate Thrash away.
  • Swap Shriekers before exceeding two DoT stacks.

Healers

  • Plan Heart, Coil, Shattering and second-break cooldowns.
  • Stagger Blight Vein breaks.
  • Clear Petrifying Sting absorbs.
  • Track Bite soakers through Purge spread.

DPS

  • No venom may touch eggs.
  • Follow side and Coil group.
  • Interrupt Anguished Cry/Vicious Echoes.
  • Fully soak Bite, then spread immediately.
Biggest wipes: Caustic Wave hatches eggs; wrong Coil group; fewer than 40% soak; simultaneous Fangs breaks; missed Cry; big egg fed before small; Bite not fully transferred; Purge stacked; late Circling Prey evacuation; Vicious Echoes cast.
CALL: Protect eggs → alternate Coils → burn Heart → split sides → break Fangs in pairs → small eggs then big → A 1/3/5, B 2/4/6 → Bite groups soak then spread → evacuate broken platform.
